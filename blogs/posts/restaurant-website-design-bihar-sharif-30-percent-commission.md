## 🤝 Honest Baat: Aggregator Chhodna Nahi, Kam Karna Hai

Zomato/Swiggy **naye customer** laate hain. Wo kaam ka hai.

Par jo customer **teesri baar** order kar raha hai — usse 30% pe kyun pakadna?

> **Repeat customer ko apne channel pe lao. Ye hai unit economics.**

| Customer Type | Kiska Channel | Commission |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Pehli baar (discovery) | Aggregator | 30% — theek hai |
| Doosri baar (repeat) | Aggregator | 30% — **nuksaan** |
| Teesri baar+ (loyal) | **Apna channel** | **0%** |
