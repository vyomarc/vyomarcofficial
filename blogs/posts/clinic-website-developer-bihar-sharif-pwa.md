## ⚙️ A Process Problem, Not a Doctor Problem

This is not a medical issue; it's a structural one. A receptionist with a phone and a register is a **single-thread system**. They can only handle one booking at a time. Everyone else gets a busy tone. And when patients just show up, they create a **zero-queue system** where everyone waits in a chaotic blob. 

Server-side logic fixes this by managing the queue digitally, asynchronously, and perfectly.

## 📱 What is a PWA (In Plain Language)?

A Progressive Web App (PWA) looks and feels like a native mobile app but works directly in the browser. 
- **No App Store needed:** The patient doesn't have to download a 50MB app from the Play Store.
- **Instant access:** They click a link (from your Google Business Profile or WhatsApp), and it opens instantly.
- **Lightweight:** It works perfectly on slow 4G connections common in Tier-2 cities.
- **Installable:** They can tap "Add to Home Screen" to get an app icon for their next visit.

## 🔄 How the PWA System Works Step by Step

1. **The Link:** Patient clicks the clinic link on Google or WhatsApp.
2. **The Slot:** They see the doctor's live availability and pick an open slot.
3. **The Booking:** They enter their name and phone number.
4. **The Token:** The server immediately sends a WhatsApp confirmation with a token number and expected time.
5. **The Arrival:** The patient arrives 10 minutes before their slot. The waiting room stays uncrowded.
