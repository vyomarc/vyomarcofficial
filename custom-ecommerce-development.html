Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about custom e-commerce architecture, pricing, and integrations.

Is this better than Shopify for my business?

Yes, if you want zero recurring platform fees, complete ownership of your code and customer data, lightning-fast load times, and an ordering process tuned specifically for Indian buyers who prefer direct WhatsApp communication over complex multi-step checkout forms.

How does WhatsApp checkout work?

When a customer adds items to their shopping cart and clicks checkout, the store automatically generates a clean, itemized order summary with product titles, quantities, prices, and total amount, opening WhatsApp with the message ready to send directly to your business number.

How do I update products and prices?

We provide a secure, lightweight admin dashboard where you can easily add new products, update prices, upload images, manage categories, and toggle stock availability without touching any code.

Can I accept online payments like UPI and Cards?

Yes. Razorpay, PhonePe, or Paytm payment gateway integration can be added seamlessly so customers can pay instantly via UPI apps (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit or debit cards, and net banking.

How much does hosting cost if there are no monthly platform fees?

Modern static and serverless architectures allow hosting on platforms like Vercel or Netlify for free during your launch stage. For high-volume stores requiring custom VPS servers, hosting typically costs between 300 to 500 rupees per month, saving thousands compared to SaaS subscription tiers.
