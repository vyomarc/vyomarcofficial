Why Zomato & Swiggy Commissions are Destroying Your Restaurant Business?

In India's hyper-competitive food industry, profit margins for dine-in restaurants, cafes, and cloud kitchens average between 12% to 18%. When aggregators charge 22% to 30% on gross order value, restaurants are effectively operating at breakeven or running negative cash flows on digital delivery.

Beyond high commission cuts, restaurants face hidden aggregator costs including customer data masking (you never own your customer's phone number or email), mandatory platform-funded discounts, onboarding fees, payment gateway splits, and bidding against competitors for local search visibility.

How to Calculate Your Commission Loss

To calculate your true losses to aggregators, use this verified business math:

Daily Loss = (Average Order Value × Commission Rate %) ÷ 100 × Daily Orders
Monthly Loss = Daily Loss × 30 Days
Annual Loss = Monthly Loss × 12 Months

For example, a restaurant processing 30 orders a day with a modest average bill of ₹400 on a 25% commission platform gives away ₹3,000 every single day, resulting in ₹90,000 lost per month and a staggering ₹10,80,000 lost every year. That is over 10 Lakhs in retained profit that could fund an entire new outlet or marketing campaign.
