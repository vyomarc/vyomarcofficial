What is the Percentage to CGPA Formula for Indian Students?

In the Indian academic system governed by CBSE, AICTE, and state technical universities, Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is converted into equivalent aggregate percentage by multiplying the CGPA by a standard factor of 9.5. This factor was scientifically derived by analyzing the 5-year average percentage scores of the top 100 students across previous board examinations.

10-Point Scale (CBSE, AICTE, VTU, AKTU)

Percentage (%) = CGPA × 9.5

Example: If your CGPA is 8.4, your equivalent percentage is 8.4 × 9.5 = 79.80%. To convert back from percentage to CGPA, divide your percentage by 9.5.

7-Point Scale (Mumbai University & UGC)

Percentage (%) = (CGPA - 0.75) × 10

Example: If your 7-point CGPA is 5.5, your equivalent percentage is (5.5 - 0.75) × 10 = 47.50%. Standard for Mumbai University arts, commerce, and technology faculties.
