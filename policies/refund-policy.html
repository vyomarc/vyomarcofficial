Refund & Cancellation Policy

VYOMARC Technologies

Effective Date: November 2026

At VYOMARC Technologies, we believe in complete transparency. Our web development, app development, and software engineering projects require dedicated time, resources, and technical infrastructure. To ensure a smooth collaboration, please read our onboarding, payment, and cancellation terms carefully.

1. Project Onboarding & Payment Structure

We follow a strict milestone-based onboarding and deployment process:

  • Domain Ownership: The client is entirely responsible for purchasing and providing the Domain Name (e.g., .com, .in). VYOMARC Technologies does not purchase domains on behalf of clients to ensure you retain 100% legal ownership of your web address.
  • Project Kickoff (Advance Payment): Before any design or development work begins, the client must sign the project agreement and pay a minimum 40% advance of the total development charge, plus 100% of the 1st-year hosting/server charges (if applicable).
  • Fixed Scope & Additional Features: The advance payment covers only the features and functionalities explicitly listed in the original project agreement. Any new feature requests, modifications, or scope expansions (e.g., requesting e-commerce capabilities later) requested after 48 hours of project initiation will be evaluated and billed separately.
  • Project Handover (Final Payment): The remaining 60% of the development charge must be cleared in full before the final project is handed over, deployed to the live server, or the master source code (ZIP) is delivered.

2. Standard Payment Modes (No Service Exchange)

As a standardized IT agency, all financial settlements must be executed via official commercial channels: Cash, Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS), or UPI. We do not accept or entertain barter systems or service-exchange agreements for our tech development.

3. Cancellation & Refund Slabs

We understand that business plans can change. If you wish to cancel your project, refunds will be processed based on the project's timeline and completion stage:

  • Cancellation within 24 Hours of Agreement & Advance Payment: 100% refund of the development advance + 100% refund of hosting charges. (Reason: The project is still in the administrative queue, and no active engineering hours or server purchases have been initiated).
  • Cancellation between 24 to 48 Hours: 50% refund of the development advance + 100% refund of hosting charges. (Reason: By the 24-hour mark, our team actively initiates project setup, allocates engineering resources, and begins UI/UX wireframing. The 50% deduction compensates for these irreversible administrative and initial labor costs).
  • Cancellation at 30% - 50% Project Completion: 0% refund of the development advance + 100% refund of hosting charges. (Reason: Software development is highly labor-intensive. By this phase, our engineers have dedicated substantial hours to writing core code, structuring databases, and building the web architecture. Development time, once spent, cannot be recovered or resold. However, since the final live deployment hasn't occurred, hosting charges remain unused and are fully refunded).
  • Cancellation Post-Completion or Project Abandonment: No refunds will be issued (0% development refund, 0% hosting refund). (Reason: Once the project is fully developed, 100% of the agreed-upon engineering labor has been consumed. Furthermore, the hosting and server infrastructure is permanently allocated and purchased from our cloud providers to prepare your system for launch).

4. Third-Party Costs & Processing Timelines

  • Third-Party APIs & Licenses (Non-Refundable): Any costs incurred for third-party services purchased specifically on behalf of the client (such as WhatsApp Business API setups, SMS Gateways, premium plugins, or commercial software licenses) are strictly non-refundable under all circumstances, as these vendors do not issue refunds to us.
  • Refund Processing Time: Approved refunds will be officially processed and credited back to the client's original payment source within 5 to 7 business days.

5. Client Responsiveness & Project Abandonment

If a project is stalled for more than 30 days due to a lack of feedback, assets (images/text/content), or communication from the client's end, VYOMARC Technologies reserves the right to mark the project as "Abandoned." No refunds will be issued for abandoned projects.
