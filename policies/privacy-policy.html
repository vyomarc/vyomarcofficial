Privacy Policy

VYOMARC Technologies

Effective Date: November 2026

At VYOMARC Technologies, we are strictly committed to safeguarding your privacy. As a digital infrastructure and software engineering firm, we prioritize the confidentiality and integrity of the data you entrust to us while utilizing our services and web platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We practice data minimization, collecting only the information strictly necessary to engineer solutions and provide our services. This includes:

  • Client Communication Data: Personal identifiers such as your name, email address, and phone number submitted voluntarily through our contact forms or consultation requests.
  • Project & Technical Assets: Business requirements, credentials, digital assets, and documentation provided by you to fulfill our software development and service agreements.
  • Automated Analytics: Non-personally identifiable information (such as browser type, device information, and usage metrics) collected automatically to monitor performance and optimize our website’s user experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

We utilize your information exclusively for operational and service-oriented purposes, including:

  • Service Delivery: To design, develop, and deploy your custom software, web architecture, and automation systems.
  • Client Support: To communicate regarding project proposals, updates, technical support, and billing transactions.
  • System Optimization: To analyze website traffic and improve our internal operations and service offerings.
  • Zero Data Sale Policy: VYOMARC Technologies does not sell, rent, or lease your personal or business data to any third-party marketing entities or aggregators.

3. Data Security Protocols

We implement robust administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or disclosure. While we utilize industry-standard security protocols to secure our digital environments, please note that no method of digital transmission or electronic storage over the internet can be guaranteed as 100% secure.
